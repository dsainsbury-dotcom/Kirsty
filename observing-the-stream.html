Observing the Stream

Kirsty session · 28 September 2026 · around 2–3 minutes

The point

My mind does not need to go quiet. The practice is to step back from the stream of thoughts, notice what is happening, and let the chatter carry on without having to fight it or follow every thought.

What to notice

  • Where the thoughts seem to be: in my head, body or somewhere else.
  • Whether they seem still or moving, and whether the pace changes.
  • Whether they show up as words, pictures, sounds, objects or a mixture.
  • Whether there are gaps or pauses between thoughts.
  • What happens when I stop trying to control the stream and simply let it be there.

The useful bit

The aim is not to stop thinking. It is to notice the thought stream, make room for it, and carry on with life while it does its thing in the background.
